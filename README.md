🧠 Risk Intelligence

Instead of simply saying:

❌ “This land is fake.”

PlotShield AI can surface specific risk indicators requiring investigation.

Example:

⚠️ RISK INDICATORS

• Document information requires review
• Property details need confirmation
• Seller information incomplete
• Location information requires verification

ACTION:
Consult a qualified professional before purchase.

This makes the platform more responsible and useful.

⚠️ Important Disclaimer

PlotShield AI is a technology and decision-support platform.

AI-generated information, property scores, extracted document data, estimates, and risk indicators are not legal title certification, ownership guarantees, or professional legal advice.

Property buyers should independently verify records and consult qualified legal professionals and relevant authorities before completing a land transaction.
